# Puddle

> Puddle is a technical-interview platform for evaluating software-engineering candidates in real-world, AI-enabled conditions. Candidates work through live coding assessments in a sandboxed IDE-and-terminal environment and may use modern AI tools during the session; Puddle scores how they approach problems across structured phases and produces an outcome report with hiring recommendations. Sold as a B2B SaaS recruitment-assessment tool. As of this profile Puddle publishes no public API, developer documentation, SDK, or OpenAPI surface.
